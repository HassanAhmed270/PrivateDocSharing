## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q: Does the signature drawing canvas work on touchscreens and mobile devices?**  
A: Yes! The canvas component listens to both mouse events (`mousedown`, `mousemove`) and touch gestures (`touchstart`, `touchmove`), making it smooth on iPads, tablets, and smartphones.

**Q: How is the signature stored?**  
A: The canvas exports the drawing as a lightweight Base64 PNG image string (`data:image/png;base64,...`) which can be saved directly in MongoDB or PostgreSQL.
